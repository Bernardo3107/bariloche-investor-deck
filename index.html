Distribution channels

Three ways guests will find the house

Online platforms

≈ 65% of bookings at launch

  • Airbnb, aiming for Luxe
  • Booking.com
  • Vrbo and Expedia
  • Google Vacation Rentals

Direct bookings

Target 30–40% by year 2

  • Own site with booking engine
  • Instagram and WhatsApp
  • Repeat-guest offers
  • Referrals and email list

Partners

Groups and long stays

  • Travel agents in Brazil and Buenos Aires
  • Ski schools and tour operators
  • Corporate retreats
  • Local rental agencies

A channel manager (e.g. Hostaway, Lodgify) syncs calendars and prices across all channels and avoids double bookings.

Platforms list Bariloche group homes today; local agencies such as Los Cerros Propiedades and Coldwell Banker Patagonia Lakes also run temporary rentals.

Marketing strategy

From first photo to repeat guests

Months 0–2

Prepare

  • Brand name and logo
  • Pro photos, drone and video
  • Website in PT, ES and EN

Months 2–4

Launch

  • List on all platforms
  • Launch rates 10–15% lower to win the first 20 reviews
  • Google Business Profile

Months 4–12

Grow

  • Instagram and TikTok reels: snow, lake, fireplace
  • Meta and Google ads in São Paulo, Buenos Aires, Mexico
  • Family-travel creator stays

Year 2 onward

Scale direct

  • 5–10% off for booking direct
  • Packages: ski passes, private chef, boat tours
  • WhatsApp and email CRM

Targets: 4.9★ rating · 50%+ yearly occupancy · 30–40% direct bookings · ad budget ≈ USD 250 per month
